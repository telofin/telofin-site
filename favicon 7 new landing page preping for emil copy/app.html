Log donation
Non-cash gifts over $500 require the donor to file Form 8283. Over $5,000 requires a qualified appraisal.
In-kind donations are non-cash gifts (donated goods, services, or use of property). Enter the fair market value as established by the donor — do not assert FMV in the donor acknowledgment letter. Reported separately on Form 990 and not counted toward Schedule B cash thresholds.
—
Quid pro quo: when a donor receives goods or services in exchange for their gift (e.g. a gala dinner, auction item), only the amount above the fair market value of what they received is tax-deductible. This will be noted on their thank you letter.

Record restriction release

Records a reclassification of net assets from restricted to unrestricted per GAAP ASC 958-205. Use when restricted or endowment funds are spent according to donor intent.

Record interfund transfer

Moves money between funds without affecting income or expenses. Appears in the GL and Fund P&L as a transfer entry.
